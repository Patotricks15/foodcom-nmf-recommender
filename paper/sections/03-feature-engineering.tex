\section{Feature Engineering}
Features are declared in \texttt{conf/features.yaml} and implemented in
\texttt{src/recommender/features/}, following the same declarative
registry pattern used throughout this portfolio: a feature cannot be
computed without being declared, and a declaration cannot go unimplemented.
Every point-in-time feature receives an explicit cut-off timestamp and is
covered by adversarial leakage tests that forge future interactions,
compare cut-offs, and scramble the training association to confirm the
pipeline cannot read what it should not see.

User features capture activity volume, average rating generosity, taste
tenure, and recency relative to the cut-off. Recipe features capture
catalogue-wide and trailing popularity, category, and -- distinct from the
companion MovieLens and anime projects -- a direct passthrough of nutrition
facts (calories, sodium). The nutrition fields are deliberately excluded
from the collaborative model's training matrix: they are consumed only by
the post-hoc filter described in Section~\ref{sec:methodology}, so the
dietary constraint remains independent of whatever the factorization has
learned from review co-occurrence.
