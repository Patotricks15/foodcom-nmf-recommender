\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
NMF is additive and inexpensive, and we have shown its latent components
admit a non-negative "flavor profile" reading that signed factorizations
cannot offer, but it does not model temporal drift inside the
factorization and offers only a popularity fallback for cold users, the
same limitation PureSVD carries in the companion MovieLens project. The
nutrition filter is a hard post-filter rather than a learned constraint,
which is a deliberate safety choice but means its interaction with ranking
quality must be measured explicitly, as we do in Section~\ref{sec:results},
rather than assumed away. Results on the synthetic fixture are not claims
about the full 1.4M-review Food.com dataset. The project demonstrates a
leakage-resistant, tested classical recommendation pipeline that combines
an interpretable collaborative model with an auditable domain constraint,
rather than a claim that NMF or any single nutrition-filter setting is
optimal.
